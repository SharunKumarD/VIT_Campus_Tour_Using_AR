## What the app uses, and why

| Data | Used for | Stored? | Sent anywhere? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your settings (step-free, pace…) | Choosing routes | On this phone | No |
| Location (GPS) | Showing where you are, starting a route from your position | **No.** Kept in memory only while the map or "My location" is open | No |
| Demo map position (only if you press "Place demo map at my location") | Testing the demo map outdoors | On this phone, until you move it back | No |
| Camera images | AR tracking and reading checkpoint QR codes | **No.** Processed live, never saved | No |
| AR session logs (timings, distances, guidance events) | Research evaluation | On this phone, until you delete them | Only if you copy and share them |

Session logs contain no name, account or GPS coordinates.

## What the app does not do

- No account, advertising, analytics or tracking.
- No background location.
- No data is sent over the internet by this build.
- QR codes are only matched against known marker IDs; they are never opened as links.

## Your choices

- Deny location or camera: the map and text directions still work.
- Delete research sessions: Settings > Research sessions > Delete all sessions.
- Reset settings: Settings > Reset settings.
- Remove everything: uninstall the app.

## Research participation

If you take part in a study, you will be asked for informed consent first,
under the approval of VIT's Institutional Ethics Committee. You may stop at any
time. Logs are shared with researchers only when you choose to copy them.

## Changes

This notice will be updated before any feature that sends data off the phone
(for example a hosted campus-data server or an AI assistant).
